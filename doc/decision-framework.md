# Decision Framework

## Core Decision
For each application, recommend one of four actions:
- Retain
- Retire
- Modernize
- Consolidate

## Decision Inputs
- Business criticality
- User adoption
- Annual cost
- Lifecycle status
- Technical debt
- Security risk
- Support burden
- Duplicate functionality
- Replacement candidate availability

## Decision Rules
### Retain
Use when the application has strong business value, acceptable risk, and no near-term replacement pressure.

### Retire
Use when the application has low usage, low criticality, high cost or support risk, and limited business dependency.

### Modernize
Use when the application is business-critical but has aging technology, technical debt, security exposure, or support burden.

### Consolidate
Use when the application overlaps with another system and a viable replacement or target platform exists.

## AI Role
AI supports the decision by analyzing signals, generating a recommendation, and explaining rationale.

## Human Role
Humans approve, modify, or reject the recommendation. AI does not make the final decision.
